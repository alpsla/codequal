## 💼 Equity Structure

### Pre-Money Cap Table
```
Founder (You):              75%
Marketing Co-Founder:       25%
-----------------------------------
Total Outstanding:         100%

Option Pool (Reserved):     10% (dilutes both founders equally)
```

### Post-Option Pool Cap Table
```
Founder (You):              67.5%  (75% × 90%)
Marketing Co-Founder:       22.5%  (25% × 90%)
Option Pool:                10%
-----------------------------------
Total:                     100%
```

### Vesting Schedule

**Standard 4-Year Vesting with 1-Year Cliff:**

| Timeline | Vested Equity | Cliff Protection |
|----------|---------------|------------------|
| **Month 0-11** | 0% | Nothing vested until 12 months |
| **Month 12** | 25% (6.25% absolute) | First cliff vests |
| **Month 13-48** | Linear monthly (2.08% per month) | Gradual vesting |
| **Month 48** | 100% (25% absolute) | Fully vested |

**Example Vesting Timeline:**
- **Before Month 12:** Co-founder leaves → Gets 0%
- **At Month 12:** Co-founder leaves → Gets 6.25% (25% of 25%)
- **At Month 24:** Co-founder leaves → Gets 12.5% (50% of 25%)
- **At Month 48+:** Co-founder stays → Gets full 25%

**Acceleration Clauses:**
- **Single-Trigger Acceleration:** None (protects acquirer)
- **Double-Trigger Acceleration:** 50% vesting acceleration if:
  1. Company acquired AND
  2. Co-founder terminated without cause within 12 months

## 🗳️ Decision-Making & Governance

### Board Composition (Initial)
```
Total Board Seats:         2
Founder (CEO):             1 seat
Marketing Co-Founder:      1 seat
```

**Board Votes Required:**
- **Unanimous (2/2 votes):** Fundraising, acquisition, dissolving company, issuing new equity
- **Majority (1/2 votes):** Hiring, firing, budgets, strategic decisions

**Post-Funding Board Composition:**
```
Total Board Seats:         5
Founder (CEO):             2 seats
Marketing Co-Founder:      1 seat
Investor Representatives:  2 seats
```

### Deadlock Resolution
If founders disagree on a critical decision:
1. **Cooling-off Period:** 7 days to discuss and find compromise
2. **Mediation:** Third-party advisor (YC partner, mutual mentor)
3. **CEO Tiebreaker:** If still deadlocked, Founder (CEO) has final say on product/tech decisions, CMO has final say on marketing/sales decisions

## 🚪 Exit & Termination Provisions

### Voluntary Departure (Co-Founder Quits)

**Before 1-Year Cliff:**
- Co-founder receives: 0% equity
- All unvested shares return to company
- Non-compete applies for 6 months

**After 1-Year Cliff:**
- Co-founder receives: All vested shares
- Unvested shares return to company
- Non-compete applies for 12 months
- Company has Right of First Refusal (ROFR) on vested shares

### Involuntary Termination (For Cause)

**For Cause Reasons:**
- Fraud, embezzlement, or theft
- Gross negligence or willful misconduct
- Breach of fiduciary duty
- Criminal conviction
- Material breach of agreement

**Consequences:**
- Co-founder forfeits ALL equity (vested and unvested)
- Immediate termination
- No severance

### Involuntary Termination (Without Cause)

**Without Cause Reasons:**
- Underperformance (not meeting KPIs)
- Cultural misfit
- Company pivot makes role redundant
- Founder decides to go solo again

**Consequences:**
- Co-founder keeps all vested equity
- Unvested equity returns to company
- 3-month severance (if after Month 12)
- Company ROFR on vested shares

### Death or Disability

**Disability Definition:** Unable to work 30+ hours/week for 90 consecutive days

**Consequences:**
- Vesting continues for 12 months (compassion provision)
- After 12 months, unvested shares return to company
- Vested shares transfer to estate/heirs
- Company ROFR applies

## 🔒 Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment

### IP Ownership

**All IP created by either founder belongs to CodeQual, Inc.**

**Covered IP:**
- Code, algorithms, documentation
- Marketing materials, content, branding
- Inventions, patents, trade secrets
- Customer lists, business strategies

**Prior IP Exclusions:**
- Co-founder may exclude pre-existing IP created before joining
- Must be documented in writing at signing
- No overlap with CodeQual's business

### Work-for-Hire

All work performed during the co-founder relationship is **work-for-hire** and owned by the company, even if:
- Created outside normal working hours
- Created using personal equipment
- Not directly related to current product

## 🚫 Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation

### Non-Compete (During Employment)

**Prohibited Activities:**
- Starting or joining a direct competitor
- Advising or investing in a competitor
- Using CodeQual trade secrets for another business

**Geographic Scope:** Worldwide (reasonable for SaaS)
**Industry Scope:** AI code analysis, static code analysis, developer tools

### Non-Compete (Post-Employment)

**Duration:** 12 months after departure (if after cliff)
**Scope:** Cannot start or join a direct competitor
**Exception:** If fired without cause, reduced to 6 months

### Non-Solicitation (Post-Employment)

**Duration:** 18 months after departure
**Prohibited Actions:**
- Soliciting CodeQual employees to leave
- Soliciting CodeQual customers to switch
- Soliciting CodeQual investors or partners

**Exception:** General job postings are allowed (but no direct recruiting)

## 🔄 Right of First Refusal (ROFR) & Co-Sale

### ROFR (Company's Right)

If co-founder wants to sell vested shares:
1. Must notify company in writing
2. Company has 30 days to match offer
3. If company declines, other co-founder has 30 days
4. If both decline, co-founder can sell to third party

**Purpose:** Prevents random outsiders from owning equity early on

### Co-Sale Rights (Tag-Along)

If Founder (CEO) sells 50%+ of their shares:
- Co-founder has right to sell proportional shares at same price
- Example: CEO sells 50% stake → CMO can sell 50% of their stake
- Protects minority co-founder from being left behind

**Exception:** Does not apply to sales to family members or trusts

## 💸 Funding & Dilution Protection

### Future Fundraising

**Both founders agree:**
- Company may raise capital from VCs, angels, or strategic investors
- Both founders will dilute equally (pro-rata)
- Option pool may be increased to 15-20% for hiring

**Example Dilution (Series A at $5M valuation, 20% sold):**
```
Before Series A:
Founder (CEO):            67.5%
Marketing Co-Founder:     22.5%
Option Pool:              10%

After Series A (20% to investors):
Founder (CEO):            54%    (67.5% × 80%)
Marketing Co-Founder:     18%    (22.5% × 80%)
Option Pool:              8%     (10% × 80%)
Investors:                20%
```

### Anti-Dilution Protection

**Standard Terms:**
- **Weighted Average Anti-Dilution:** Protects against down rounds
- Applies to both founders equally
- Common in VC term sheets

## ⚖️ Fair Terms Checklist

Use this checklist to ensure terms are fair for both parties:

**For Founder (CEO):**
- [ ] Protected by 1-year cliff (co-founder must prove commitment)
- [ ] For-cause termination forfeits all equity (prevents bad actors)
- [ ] ROFR protects against unwanted shareholders
- [ ] Maintains control through board seats and tiebreaker

- [ ] 25% equity is fair for time + expertise (no cash)
