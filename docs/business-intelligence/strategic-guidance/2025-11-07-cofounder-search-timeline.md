## 🎯 Search Strategy Overview

**Candidates:** Interview 10-15, deep dive with 3-5, trial with 2-3, offer to 1

### Week 6 (Mid-December): Phone Screens

**Goal:** Narrow to 5-7 finalists for deep interviews

**Process:**
Each candidate gets 30-minute phone screen

**Schedule:**
- Monday-Wednesday: 2 screens/day = 6 total
- Thursday-Friday: 2 screens/day = 4 total
- **Total:** 10 phone screens

**Phone Screen Questions:**

**1. Background (5 minutes)**
- "Walk me through your career journey in 2 minutes"
- "What's your B2B SaaS marketing experience?"
- "Tell me about a product you grew from 0 → 1,000 users"

**2. Why This? (5 minutes)**
- "Why co-founder vs full-time employee?"
- "What excites you about CodeQual specifically?"
- "What concerns do you have about this opportunity?"

**3. Expertise Check (10 minutes)**
- "What marketing channel do you dominate?"
- "How would you get our first 1,000 users in 90 days?"
- "What's your approach to content marketing for developers?"

**4. Logistics (5 minutes)**
- "How many hours/week can you commit Year 1?"
- "What's your financial runway? (Can you work for equity Year 1?)"
- "What are you looking for in a co-founder?"

**5. Q&A (5 minutes)**
- Their questions for you
- Gauge curiosity, thoughtfulness

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- ✅ Green Light: Impressive experience + culture fit + excited
- 🟡 Maybe: Good but need more data → strategy interview
- ❌ No: Missing key experience or red flags

**Target:** Invite 5-7 to strategy interviews (Week 7)

### Week 7 (Late December): Strategy Interviews

**Goal:** Deep dive with finalists, narrow to 2-3 for trial

**Process:**
60-minute video interview with each finalist

**Schedule:**
- Monday-Friday: 1 interview/day = 5 total
- Weekend: Review notes, pick top 3

**Strategy Interview Agenda:**

**Part 1: Deep Dive on Experience (20 min)**
- "Tell me about your biggest marketing win"
- "Walk me through how you grew [previous product]"
- "What marketing channels have you mastered?"
- "How do you think about content for technical audiences?"

**Part 2: Strategic Thinking (20 min)**

**Scenario 1:** "We have $5,000 to spend on user acquisition in Month 1. How would you allocate it?"

**Scenario 2:** "ProductHunt launch is in 2 weeks. What's your plan to get #1 Product of the Day?"

**Scenario 3:** "We're struggling to convert free → paid (only 2% conversion). What's your diagnostic process?"

**Part 3: Working Together (10 min)**
- "How do you like to communicate? (Async vs sync?)"
- "How do you handle disagreements?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to pivot strategy"
- "What are your expectations of me as technical co-founder?"

**Part 4: Vision Alignment (10 min)**
- "Where do you want to be in 5 years?"
- "What's your dream outcome for CodeQual?"
- "Are you looking for lifestyle business or unicorn attempt?"
- "How do you think about fundraising? (Bootstrap vs VC?)"

**After Each Interview:**
- Score candidate (1-10) on:
  - Marketing expertise: _/10
  - Strategic thinking: _/10
  - Culture fit: _/10
  - Communication: _/10
  - Vision alignment: _/10
  - Total: _/50

**Target:** Top 3 candidates proceed to trial (Week 8)

### Week 8 (Late December/Early January): Trial Projects

**Goal:** Work with 2-3 finalists, pick #1

**Trial Structure:**
- **Duration:** 10-15 hours over 1-2 weeks
- **Compensation:** $2,000-3,000 (paid regardless of outcome)
- **Format:** Real project with specific deliverables

**Trial Project Brief:**

**Scenario:** CodeQual is launching GitHub App on ProductHunt in 4 weeks. Create the launch plan.

**Deliverables (Due: End of Week 8):**

1. **ProductHunt Launch Plan** (Doc)
   - Pre-launch strategy (2 weeks before)
   - Launch day timeline (hour-by-hour plan)
   - Post-launch follow-up
   - Target: #1 Product of the Day

2. **Content Strategy** (Doc)
   - 10 blog post topics for first month
   - SEO keyword research (which terms to target)
   - Content distribution plan (where to post, when)

3. **Sample Blog Posts** (3 posts)
   - Technical deep dive: "How CodeQual Analyzes Code 50× Cheaper Than Competitors"
   - Educational: "5 Code Review Mistakes That Cost Your Team $50k/Year"
   - Competitive: "SonarQube vs CodeQual: Complete Comparison"

4. **90-Day User Acquisition Plan** (Spreadsheet + Doc)
   - Channels to test (free + paid)
   - Budget allocation ($5,000 total)
   - Expected CAC, conversion rates, user targets
   - Key metrics to track

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- **Quality of thinking** (Is strategy sound?)
- **Attention to detail** (Is execution plan thorough?)
- **Writing ability** (Can they explain technical concepts?)
- **Creativity** (Do they find non-obvious opportunities?)
- **Cultural fit** (How's communication during trial?)

**During Trial:**
- Daily async check-ins (Slack/Discord)
- 2-3 video calls to discuss progress
- See how they work, communicate, problem-solve

**After Trial:**
- Debrief with each candidate (feedback both ways)
- Make decision within 48 hours
- Send offer to #1, let others know (with grace)

## 📋 Candidate Sourcing Strategies

### Strategy 1: Warm Network (Highest Success Rate)

**Who to Contact:**
1. **Former Colleagues**
   - Marketing people you've worked with and respect
   - People who'd run through walls for you
   - Bonus: Already trust each other, no cold start

2. **University/Bootcamp Alumni**
   - Marketing grads from your school
   - Alumni working at dev tools companies
   - Use alumni network/LinkedIn

3. **Conference/Meetup Contacts**
   - People you met at conferences, clicked with
   - Reach back out: "Hey, want to see what I'm building?"

**Outreach Template (Warm):**

## 🚫 Red Flags to Watch For

**During Phone Screen:**
- ❌ Can't explain their marketing experience clearly
- ❌ Asks about salary before asking about product
- ❌ Talks more than listens (ego)
- ❌ No questions for you (not curious)
- ❌ Doesn't understand what B2B SaaS is

**During Strategy Interview:**
- ❌ Vague answers ("I'd just do marketing")
- ❌ No knowledge of developer audiences
- ❌ Can't articulate how they've grown a product before
- ❌ Wants 50% equity (unreasonable expectations)
- ❌ Unwilling to do trial project (not confident in skills)

**During Trial Project:**
- ❌ Misses deadlines without communication
- ❌ Low-quality work (typos, shallow thinking)
- ❌ Doesn't ask clarifying questions
- ❌ Defensive when given feedback
- ❌ Poor communication (slow to respond)

**General Red Flags:**
- ❌ Has 3+ other side projects (won't focus)
- ❌ Can't commit 20+ hrs/week (need real work)
- ❌ Doesn't believe in vesting (wants free lunch)
- ❌ Wants CEO title (ego problem)
- ❌ Bad-mouths former employers (culture risk)

## ✅ Green Flags to Look For

**Positive Signals:**
- ✅ Has grown a B2B SaaS from 0 → 1,000+ users before
- ✅ Can show portfolio of high-quality content (blog, videos)
- ✅ Asks intelligent questions about product and market
- ✅ Excited about education angle (not just "cheaper SonarQube")
- ✅ Understands developer audiences (ideally can code)
- ✅ Has personal brand (Twitter following, blog readers)
- ✅ Clear communication style (writes well, speaks clearly)
- ✅ Self-starter mentality (doesn't wait for direction)
- ✅ Data-driven (talks about metrics, experiments, learning)
- ✅ Realistic expectations (knows Year 1 is equity-only)

**Best Case Scenario:**
- ✅ Previously CMO or marketing lead at dev tool startup
- ✅ Grew a product to $100k+ MRR
- ✅ Has network in dev community (can bring partnerships)
- ✅ Can start immediately or within 2 weeks
- ✅ Financial runway to work for equity 12+ months
- ✅ Excited about long-term (4-year+ commitment)
- ✅ Values-aligned (education mission, developer empathy)

## 💡 Interview Pro Tips

**1. Don't Oversell**
- Be honest about challenges (solo founder, no funding yet, zero users)
- Red flag if they're not asking hard questions
- You want someone who sees problems and still says yes

**2. Assess Culture Fit Early**
- Can you imagine working with this person for 4 years?
- Do they energize you or drain you?
- Trust your gut (it's usually right)

**3. Check References**
- Talk to 2-3 people who've worked with them
- Ask: "Would you work with them again?"
- Ask: "What's their superpower? What's their weakness?"

**4. Look for Complementary Skills**
- Don't hire someone just like you
- Find strengths where you're weak
- You: Product/tech. Them: Marketing/GTM

**5. Test Working Style During Trial**
- How do they communicate? (Async vs sync?)
- How do they handle feedback?
- Do they ask clarifying questions or assume?
- Do they over-deliver or under-deliver?

## 🚨 Backup Plan

**If No Good Candidates by Week 9:**

**Option A: Extend Search**
- Continue search for 2-4 more weeks
- Lower bar slightly (2+ years experience vs 3+)
- Expand geographic search (international)

**Option B: Apply to Accelerator**
- Apply to YC Winter 2026 (deadline: October)
- Use YC co-founder matching program
- Accept to YC (March 2026 start)
- Find co-founder through YC network

**Option C: Hire Marketing Contractor**
- Hire marketing agency/freelancer for 3 months
- Pay cash (~$5,000-10,000/month)
- Defer co-founder search to Month 6
- Continue building solo meanwhile

**Option D: Continue Solo**
- Accept solo founder status for Year 1
- Hire first marketing employee when $10k MRR
- Offer equity (5-10%) but not co-founder level
- Revisit co-founder search in Year 2

**Recommended:** Option B (YC) if no good fit by Week 10
