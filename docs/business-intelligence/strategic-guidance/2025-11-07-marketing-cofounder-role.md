## 💰 Compensation & Equity

### Equity Structure
```
Total Ownership: 25% (vesting over 4 years)
Vesting Schedule: 4-year linear vest with 1-year cliff
Cliff Protection: 0% if leave before 12 months
Monthly Vesting: 6.25% per year = 0.52% per month after cliff

Example:
Month 6:  0% vested (before cliff)
Month 12: 6.25% vested (cliff reached)
Month 18: 9.375% vested (cliff + 6 months)
Month 24: 12.5% vested (2 years)
Year 4:   25% fully vested
```

### Compensation Timeline
```
Year 1 (Months 1-12):
Salary: $0 (equity-only)
Expenses: Reimbursed (tools, travel, marketing spend)
Living: Founder has stable income, no immediate pressure

Year 2 (Months 13-24):
Salary: Market rate when profitable (target: $80-120k)
Milestone: $10k MRR = profitable, start salaries
If not profitable: Continue equity-only OR raise seed round

Year 3+:
Salary: Market rate ($120-150k+ for CMO)
Bonus: Based on MRR growth milestones
Equity: Still vesting (50-75% by Year 3)
```
