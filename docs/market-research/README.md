# Market Research & Competitive Intelligence

This directory contains market intelligence and competitive analysis reports generated by the **Market Researcher agent**.

## Directory Structure

- **`competitive-briefs/`** - Weekly competitive intelligence briefs
- **`competitor-profiles/`** - Deep dive analyses of specific competitors
- **`sentiment-analysis/`** - Monthly developer sentiment reports
- **`pricing-intelligence/`** - Competitor pricing tracking and analysis
- **`market-reports/`** - Quarterly market opportunity reports
- **`trend-analysis/`** - Industry trend analyses

## How Reports Are Generated

The Market Researcher agent automatically:
1. Monitors competitor websites, blogs, social media
2. Tracks developer sentiment on Twitter, Reddit, HN, Stack Overflow
3. Analyzes pricing changes and feature announcements
4. Synthesizes findings into actionable intelligence
5. Saves reports to appropriate subdirectories

## Invoking the Agent

```bash
# Weekly intelligence
"Market researcher, weekly competitive brief"

# Competitor analysis
"Market researcher, what's SonarQube doing lately?"
"Research GitHub Copilot Enterprise pricing"

# Developer sentiment
"What are developers saying about code review tools?"

# Specific research
"Find case studies of teams switching from SonarQube"
```

## Orchestration with Business Owner

The Market Researcher is often triggered automatically by the Strategic Business Owner agent when market intelligence is needed for strategic decisions.
